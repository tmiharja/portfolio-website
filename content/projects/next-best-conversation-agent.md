Relationship managers at a regional bank spent a large share of their time preparing for client conversations. We built an AI agent that drafts the next best conversation for each client, grounded in the bank's own data. I led use-case definition, evaluation and UAT, and the production rollout with a team of 10+ business analysts and engineers. Measured productivity uplift was around 20%.
